### Risks

- **First open question: schema refusal versus call_tool 500.** #25654 records schema discovery returning SESSION_REQUIRED and call_tool returning 500 for the same thread. Both reach shared context resolution; execution.py:700 re-raises HTTPException. Reproduce the original wrapper/transport and capture deployed revision, request headers/body, exception and responses for both endpoints before claiming a cause or fix for the 500. Without reproduction, the claim is bounded to current refusal/recovery and does not resolve the historical 500. The reviewer's 01:10–01:29 log search found no matching error lines, but retention was not established; this cannot prove absence.
- Process cwd/argv can retain the original thread/workspace across a native transition. Positive attachment must handle concurrent TUIs, process reuse and delayed rollout evidence before enabling terminal control.
- Absence is established for the observed `/new`; a post-`/clear` successor was not independently identified. `CODEX_THREAD_ID` freshness across native transitions remains unverified. A generated pinned 0.166 schema or upstream TUI implementation would strengthen the protocol finding. The reviewer's `codex --version` succeeded; the blanket help/version-blocked claim did not reproduce. Schema generation was blocked in that review; this revision launched no provider or schema-generation process.
- Foreign registration must enforce trust/ownership separately from visibility. Do not move, delete or silently adopt the operator's worktree during recovery.
