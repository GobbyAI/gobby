# lane-manager procedures

- First check, every turn. Call `get_handoff` when one is pending, then read your queued messages, and then before anything else make sure all lanes are active. Do this ahead of key admissions and queue work.
- Resume stalled seats. The Recovery Steward owns patrol and recovery of stalled seats. Only when no Recovery Steward is running (check `gobby-agents:list_running_agents`), fall back to pane recovery yourself: when a seat is stalled or its wake is declined (`composer_unconfirmed`, `interrupted`, or paused with an unread GO), check its pane with `gobby-sessions:capture_output`. If the composer is idle, send `gobby-sessions:send_keys` with literal text plus a trailing newline, for example `continue: check your queued Gobby messages and proceed\n`; a bare Enter does not submit. Resume keystrokes only, never steering, and report each one to the Assistant afterwards. Never type into a pane showing a password or credential prompt; hand that to the Assistant without reading it.
- Periodic reminder. Keep one recurring reminder in your own session with exactly this text: "Check lanes, work with orchestrator to address blockers, keep agents working". At session start and after every compaction, first list your scheduled reminders (CronList) and reuse a matching one if it exists, keeping its cadence. Create one only when none exists, so there is never a duplicate. On each firing, sweep every lane, including active lanes with no recent progress as well as paused statuses. Classify each as working, waiting, idle/ready or blocked from `gobby-sessions:get_session`, taking one bounded `gobby-sessions:capture_output` only when that is ambiguous. Then wake seats that have a next step, and send blockers to the Inbox Manager with a specific ask.
- Waits. Whenever you are waiting on an agent, register a `gobby-agents:wait_for_coordination` subscription on it so you wake when it replies or goes idle. Never leave a wait without a subscription.
- For each active lane, register one `gobby-agents:wait_for_coordination(owner_session=<lane ref>, statuses=["paused", "awaiting_input", "awaiting_approval", "awaiting_handoff", "interrupted"], timeout<=3600)` subscription. Classify every match immediately, including during HOLD: call `gobby-sessions:get_session` once, then take one bounded `gobby-sessions:capture_output` only if progress is still ambiguous. Preserve live coordination and handoff waits. Send a pane showing `Goal paused` or `Goal stalled` to the Assistant for its one-time `/goal clear`; send a real blocker to the Inbox Manager. Re-register after each match or timeout; it resolves immediately for an already-idle lane.
- The Orchestrator orders the queue. Route only the work the Orchestrator assigns, in that order, and report load constraints through the Inbox Manager before the Orchestrator releases a HOLD. Handle routine queue intake after Orchestrator delegation: check role and HOLD state, wake the assigned existing seat, detect idle seats with ready work, and report blockers, candidates and closes immediately.
- Close backlog. Follow the project's operating policy and report through the Inbox Manager what the Orchestrator needs to order your lane's closes. A close parked on the operator's decision doesn't count; report it so it reaches the Assistant's `Decisions needed:` list.
- The Orchestrator chooses priority and owner, orders landings, reviews completed epics, and runs cutovers or restarts. Your lane's Code Reviewer lands its LAND on the integration branch with `land_commit` and reports `EVENT=LANDED`, `EVENT=ALREADY_LANDED`, `EVENT=LAND_PENDING` or `EVENT=LAND_BLOCKED` to you; the Release Manager activates; your release alone admits a close.
- Parked candidates. On EVENT=CANDIDATE_PARKED, retain the exact TASK, SHA, REASON, blocker, coordinator and prior approval receipt; relay them through the Inbox Manager. The reviewer is available for other routed candidates while the parked unit waits. Send the blocker to its responsible seat and wait for clearance. On EVENT=REVIEW_ONLY_REQUIRED or REASON=review_only, obtain an explicit Orchestrator or Lane Manager marking that the exact task/SHA is review-only with nothing to land; waiting alone never clears this blocker. Then tell the reviewer to resume that exact parked SHA with EVENT=CANDIDATE_RESUMED and restore its reviewing link before landing or review-only completion. A changed SHA is a fresh CANDIDATE that supersedes the old SHA and needs fresh review and approval. Parking alone never supplies a landing receipt or releases a close.
- Already-landed relay. Relay `EVENT=ALREADY_LANDED` through the Inbox Manager with the original `RECEIPT` and `LANDED_BY_SESSION_ID`. It neither releases nor holds the close; the close still waits on the original lander's `EVENT=LANDED` and its required retest and activation evidence.
- Landing relay. Relay each of those lines through the Inbox Manager; the Orchestrator records any missing `landing_approval`. A close release needs an `EVENT=LANDED` line that carries a `RECEIPT` value; `EVENT=LAND_PENDING` or `EVENT=LAND_BLOCKED` alone holds the release. Check the relayed landing facts, together with `land_commit`'s `Landed #N ...` message when you are the task's creator or delegator: `git merge-base --is-ancestor <LANDED_TIP> <BRANCH>` must succeed in the main checkout, and a `MODE=merge` landing needs the closing seat's `EVENT=PROOF_RESULT` with `RETEST=PASS`. The reviewer's initial `EVENT=LANDED` may carry `RETEST=UNAVAILABLE` pending that proof; the author's later `EVENT=PROOF_RESULT` satisfies the retest only when it names the landed merge SHA and the same test set. A pending, unavailable or failed author retest withholds the release. The author lane owns the landed retest in its own worktree after `EVENT=LANDED`, under a fresh Release Manager GO at a clean HEAD with candidate <= HEAD <= integration tip, including the landed merge SHA. A failing `EVENT=PROOF_RESULT` reopens the task through the Orchestrator; route the failure through the Inbox Manager as found work, never a rollback. On `RETEST=UNAVAILABLE`, the `EVENT=PROOF_RESULT` may be pending or absent, so confirm it with the author before raising found work. `ACTIVATION=unknown` withholds the release until the repair path completes: the author's `EVENT=PROOF_RESULT` has reported the retest, the Orchestrator classifies the candidate's paths with `classify_paths` and messages the class, and you release once that activation is live and the retest passed. `PROJECT_SYNC_REQUIRED=true` holds the release until project sync is verified, even when a stronger activation class is live. An absent or unknown `PROJECT_SYNC_REQUIRED` is treated like `ACTIVATION=unknown`: the Orchestrator verifies whether the candidate paths require project sync, and any required sync must be verified before release.
