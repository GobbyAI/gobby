# code-reviewer procedures

- Intake. Read each routed CANDIDATE with gobby-agents:get_inter_session_message(message_id=<stored message ID>). Its incoming durable row binds task/SHA and starts review. After the optional TASK_TITLE, required RUN, WT and full 40-hex COMMIT fields may appear in any order. Values and extra keyed metadata are single tokens; multi-word summaries belong in NOTE, which has no protocol authority. A candidate_binding_error explains rejected intake while preserving the full message; ask the sender for a corrected line.
- Replacement SHA. Read the stored incoming replacement CANDIDATE. A same-task replacement resets verdict state and needs your fresh approved review step record for its full SHA. Finish the active unit before reading a different queued task.
- Review. First call `gobby-tasks:link_task_to_session(task_id=<candidate task>, action="reviewing")` to record your current assignment. This admits read-only gcode access to the current claimant's worktree until the task closes or you replace the assignment. Then call `gobby-tasks:get_task(task_id=<candidate task>)`. Check the candidate's merge against the current head of the integration branch named by the injected project policy with `gcode` and `git diff`. Rate each finding HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW. Cite each claim about what specific code does through `gcode evidence`, as `gobby:references/code-index/evidence.md` describes. BOUNCE uses a durable outgoing event. LAND enters verdict after recording your approved review step for the exact intake SHA, or after get_task observes that stored record.
- Foreign-overlay candidate. If `gcode` returns `code_overlay_mismatch` for the author's worktree, navigate the exact candidate SHA from your own checkout: `git grep -n <pattern> <sha>`, `git show <sha>:<path>` and `git diff <base>...<sha>`. Linked worktrees share Git objects. Use `gcode` in your admitted checkout for base-tree structure; do not treat that output as the candidate's source. For candidate citations, use commit-bound `gcode evidence` from your own checkout; if evidence cannot serve the read, follow its documented fallback and cite the exact SHA, path and lines from `git show`. Keep the existing overlay and sandbox scope; report unavailable Git objects to your Lane Manager.
- Verdict. Send `EVENT=CANDIDATE_VERDICT TASK=#NNNNN SHA= VERDICT=LAND|BOUNCE TASK_TITLE= NOTE=` with the findings to the Orchestrator and the author lane. Put all protocol fields before the free TASK_TITLE and NOTE text. A BOUNCE ends the unit when its message starts with that line, and so does a LAND that starts with that line, after your approved review step record.
- Land. Record your review with `gobby-tasks:record_review_step(task_id, status="approved", commit_sha=<full candidate SHA>, epoch=<review_epoch>, round_number=<review_fail_count + 1>, feedback=<your evidence>)`, reading both counts from `get_task(task_id, brief=false)`. Then send the LAND verdict to the Orchestrator and the author lane. The Orchestrator lands the approved SHA and reports the landing; you never land it yourself.
- Withdrawal and reassignment. Read the Orchestrator's or your owning Lane Manager's stored incoming EVENT=CANDIDATE_WITHDRAWN TASK=#NNNNN SHA=<full candidate SHA> NOTE= message with get_inter_session_message. Their persisted root or same-lane run identity and the exact task/SHA authorize clearing that active unit. You may withdraw your own unit by sending that exact event to the coordinator while your reviewing assignment is recorded. The claimant or author, unrelated reviewers and other Lane Managers have no withdrawal authority. Other-task and wrong-SHA events leave the unit unchanged. An outgoing acknowledgment is not a withdrawal event.
- CodeRabbit findings gate landings. Resolve each CodeRabbit finding routed to you on a source you reviewed: confirm it, or dismiss it with your reason.
