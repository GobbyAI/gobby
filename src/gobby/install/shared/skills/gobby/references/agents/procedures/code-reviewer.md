# code-reviewer procedures

- Intake. Read each routed CANDIDATE with gobby-agents:get_inter_session_message(message_id=<stored message ID>). Its incoming durable row binds task/SHA and starts review.
- Replacement SHA. Read the stored incoming replacement CANDIDATE. A same-task replacement resets verdict state and needs your fresh independent_review_approval for its full SHA. Finish the active unit before reading a different queued task.
- Review. First call `gobby-tasks:link_task_to_session(task_id=<candidate task>, action="reviewing")` to record your current assignment. This admits read-only gcode access to the current claimant's worktree until the task closes or you replace the assignment. Then call `gobby-tasks:get_task(task_id=<candidate task>)`. Stored replacement intake removes obsolete parked SHAs for the same task. Check the candidate's merge against the current head of the integration branch named by the injected project policy with `gcode` and `git diff`. Rate each finding HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW. Cite each claim about what specific code does through `gcode evidence`, as `gobby:references/code-index/evidence.md` describes. BOUNCE and PARK use durable outgoing events. LAND enters verdict after recording your independent_review_approval for the exact intake SHA, or after get_task observes that stored receipt.
- Foreign-overlay candidate. If `gcode` returns `code_overlay_mismatch` for the author's worktree, navigate the exact candidate SHA from your own checkout: `git grep -n <pattern> <sha>`, `git show <sha>:<path>` and `git diff <base>...<sha>`. Linked worktrees share Git objects. Use `gcode` in your admitted checkout for base-tree structure; do not treat that output as the candidate's source. For candidate citations, use commit-bound `gcode evidence` from your own checkout; if evidence cannot serve the read, follow its documented fallback and cite the exact SHA, path and lines from `git show`. Keep the existing overlay and sandbox scope; report unavailable Git objects to your Lane Manager.
- Verdict. Send `EVENT=CANDIDATE_VERDICT TASK=#NNNNN SHA= VERDICT=LAND|BOUNCE TASK_TITLE= NOTE=` with the findings to the Orchestrator and the author lane. Put all protocol fields before the free TASK_TITLE and NOTE text. A BOUNCE ends the unit when its message starts with that line.
- Land. Record your independent review with `gobby-tasks:record_close_receipt(task_id, kind="independent_review_approval", commit_sha=<full candidate SHA>)`, then send the LAND verdict and call `gobby-tasks-ops:land_commit(task_id, commit_sha)` on the same SHA. land_commit separately enforces the operator's landing_approval for freeze, restart or cutover class, and overlaps. Ask the coordinator for any missing approvals for that exact SHA and wait before retrying.
- Retest. A `landed: true` response means the branch moved. When `retest_required` is true, take the focused verification commands from the task's validation evidence and run them in the main checkout. Note each command and result, the `landed_tip`, the HEAD you tested and any unrelated checkout changes. Report `RETEST=UNAVAILABLE` when the evidence names no focused command you can run there.
- Receipt pending. With `receipt_pending: true`, the landing receipt failed after the ref moved. Send your Lane Manager `EVENT=LAND_PENDING TASK=#NNNNN SHA= TASK_TITLE= LANDED_TIP= NOTE=receipt_pending`, keep the unit open, and call `land_commit` again on the same SHA until a `receipt_id` returns. There is no rollback and no second candidate.
- Notification pending. Send each session listed in `notification_pending` the `Landed #N ...` line yourself; a replayed receipt does not resend it.
- Already landed. When `already_landed: true`, a `receipt_id` returns and `landed_by_session_id` names another session (neither your current session ID nor `unknown`), send your Lane Manager and the author lane `EVENT=ALREADY_LANDED TASK=#NNNNN SHA= TASK_TITLE= RECEIPT= LANDED_BY_SESSION_ID= NOTE=`. Copy the full `commit_sha`, original `receipt_id` and original `landed_by_session_id` into `SHA`, `RECEIPT` and `LANDED_BY_SESSION_ID`. Never send your own `EVENT=LANDED` for another reviewer's landing. That notice ends the unit when its message starts with that line.
- Landed. Once a response carries a `receipt_id` and either `already_landed` is false or `landed_by_session_id` equals your current session ID or is `unknown`, send your Lane Manager and the author lane `EVENT=LANDED TASK=#NNNNN SHA= TASK_TITLE= BRANCH= RECEIPT= MODE= LANDED_TIP= ACTIVATION= PROJECT_SYNC_REQUIRED= PROVENANCE= RETEST=PASS|FAIL|UNAVAILABLE|NOT_REQUIRED NOTE=`. This includes recovery or replay of your own landing; perform the required retest before reporting it. `SHA` is the full `commit_sha`. Copy `BRANCH`, `RECEIPT`, `MODE`, `LANDED_TIP`, `ACTIVATION`, `PROJECT_SYNC_REQUIRED` and `PROVENANCE` from that response's `branch`, `receipt_id`, `mode`, `landed_tip`, `activation_class`, `project_sync_required` and `provenance`. If `project_sync_required` is true, tell the Lane Manager that project workflow sync is also required, including when a stronger activation class is reported. If the field is absent, report unknown. `RETEST=NOT_REQUIRED` means `retest_required` was false. `NOTE` carries each retest command and result, the HEAD tested and any unrelated checkout changes. That notice ends the unit when its message starts with that line.
- Refusal. A `landed: false` response leaves the unit open. Send your Lane Manager `EVENT=LAND_BLOCKED TASK=#NNNNN SHA= TASK_TITLE= NOTE=` with the refusal, `missing_approvals` and `overlaps`. Send the author lane any refusal that needs an author action (`base_update_required`, `merge_conflict`, `candidate_not_linked`). Wait with `gobby-agents:wait_for_coordination` on the Lane Manager, or on the author lane for an author action, and call `land_commit` again once the blocker clears. Take a changed SHA for the same task through Replacement SHA before landing it.
- Withdrawal and reassignment. Read the Orchestrator's or your owning Lane Manager's stored incoming EVENT=CANDIDATE_WITHDRAWN TASK=#NNNNN SHA=<full candidate SHA> NOTE= message with get_inter_session_message. Their persisted root or same-lane run identity and the exact task/SHA authorize clearing that active or parked unit. You may withdraw your own unit by sending that exact event to the coordinator while your reviewing assignment is recorded. The claimant or author, unrelated reviewers and other Lane Managers have no withdrawal authority. Other-task and wrong-SHA events leave the unit unchanged. An outgoing acknowledgment is not a withdrawal event.
- Review-only LAND. When the Orchestrator or Lane Manager explicitly marks a mapping or planning candidate as review-only with nothing to land, finish its review and send the usual LAND verdict and independent approval. Then send EVENT=REVIEW_COMPLETE TASK=#NNNNN SHA=<full candidate SHA> VERDICT=LAND LANDING=NOT_REQUIRED NOTE= to the Lane Manager. This matching completion ends the unit without land_commit or a fabricated landing receipt. A normal LAND still waits for a landing or already-landed notice.
- Unmarked no-change LAND. If review finds nothing to land but the candidate has no explicit review-only marking, send EVENT=REVIEW_ONLY_REQUIRED TASK=#NNNNN SHA=<candidate_sha> NOTE= to the coordinator requesting confirmation. Park with REASON=review_only while that confirmation is pending; resume the same unit when it arrives, then follow Review-only LAND. Never infer a waiver or fabricate a landing.
- Park. A LAND blocked on approval, a base update, or review-only confirmation may send EVENT=CANDIDATE_PARKED TASK=#NNNNN SHA=<candidate_sha> REASON=approval|base_update|review_only NOTE= to its coordinator. Success saves the task/SHA in parked_candidates and clears the active unit to await, where another routed candidate can enter review and record its reviewing link. A failed send, wrong task/SHA, or other reason leaves the unit active. Keep the full blocker, coordinator and approval receipt in the message/handoff. Parking never withdraws approval or records a landing.
- Resume. Read the Orchestrator's or your owning Lane Manager's stored incoming EVENT=CANDIDATE_RESUMED TASK=<task> SHA=<parked SHA> NOTE= message, or send that exact event to your coordinator for your own recorded reviewing assignment. Only those persisted authorities may restore the exact parked unit, and only when no other unit is active. The claimant or author cannot resume it. Your matching stored independent_review_approval restores verdict; otherwise finish review. Restore the reviewing assignment before landing.
- CodeRabbit findings gate landings. Resolve each CodeRabbit finding routed to you on a source you reviewed: confirm it, or dismiss it with your reason.
